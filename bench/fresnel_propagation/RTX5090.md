The strided winner was repeated separately after correcting selection of the control row in the report collector. See raw logs for descriptor settings, bounded-input errors, overflow-stress counts, profiler counters, and sanitizer outcomes. Profiler timing is diagnostic and excluded from this table.

Reproduce: `cmake --preset msvc-multi -DFRESNEL_BENCH_CUDA_ARCH=120`, `cmake --build --preset build-Release`, then run the benchmark with `--sweep` and `--study-fused`.

Evidence: [raw logs](../../build/fresnel-rtx5090). Existing RTX 3090 numbers use a different CUDA version; cross-machine ratios are contextual.

## Validation and profiling

Release build succeeded; all 572 tests passed. Both full sweeps passed bounded-input, explicit FP32 reference, same-descriptor identity, unchanged-input, and graph-replay checks. The overflow stress case is intentionally excluded from bounded acceptance.

All 12 sanitizer checks passed: memcheck, racecheck, and synccheck for both fused winners and both large-tile configurations (X=13/Y=15). Racecheck reported zero warnings.

Profiler counters are collected from cached warmup X/Y launches. The standalone-transpose layout also incurs two transpose launches excluded from this kernel table but included in pipeline timing. Shared staging reduces the complete pipeline cost on this GPU. The two fused timing ranges overlap; the observed median favors Y shared staging. Desktop WDDM activity and ordinary clock variability remain sources of timing noise.
